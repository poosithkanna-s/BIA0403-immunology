University Biotechnology Project

Immunological Disease Diagnosis and Antigen Interaction Analysis System

A small computational demonstration for exploring immunological patterns, protein sequences and sequence-based interaction estimates.

01 Diagnosis

Enter symptoms and selected laboratory values to obtain a transparent educational prediction.

02 Antigen Analysis

Inspect protein length, hydrophobicity, hydrophilic regions and an antigenicity estimate.

03 Interaction

Compare two sequences using a simple composition-based compatibility estimate.

Disclaimer: This application is intended for educational and research purposes only. Results are computational predictions and must not be considered a medical diagnosis or a substitute for professional medical advice.
